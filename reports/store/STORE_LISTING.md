## Full description (draft)
> Build a squad of elemental monsters and watch them brawl in fast, deterministic auto-battles.
>
> • **Collect & evolve** a roster of fire, water, nature, and lightning creatures.
> • **Three arena sizes** — 1v1, 2v2, 3v3 — plus a full-team Brawl and a Tag mode where fallen
>   fighters rotate in.
> • **Readable combat** — element-signature skills, ultimate ceremonies, combo counters, and clear
>   HP/KO reads, all in portrait one-handed play.
> • **No PvP, no gacha, no pay-to-win.** Just collect, team-build, and watch the fights.

## Feature bullets (for the listing)
- Deterministic, tested battle core
- Element identity: fire / water / nature / lightning skills read by shape, not just colour
- Ultimate ceremonies, KO cinematics, dynamic battle music
- Tag mode + Brawl mode + sized arenas
- Clean one-handed portrait UI
