## Full description (≤4000 chars)
Raise your own team of monsters and watch them clash in fast, cinematic auto-battles!

Pick three monsters, send them into a chaotic "tawuran" brawl, and watch dashes, combos,
launchers, slams and screen-shaking ultimates play out — then climb the career ladder.

FEATURES
• Collect 21 unique monsters across Fire, Water and Nature — each with its own role.
• A living, reactive battle: element arenas, impact reactions, dust, cracks and shockwaves.
• Level up and TRAIN your monsters; EVOLVE them into stronger forms.
• Build the perfect team using the Fire → Nature → Water advantage triangle.
• CAREER mode: six leagues, eighteen stages of rising difficulty.
• DAILY rewards, QUESTS, and ACHIEVEMENTS to chase.
• A full MONSTER DEX encyclopedia — discover them all.
• 100% offline. No account required. Plays great on the go.

Deterministic battle engine — the same team and seed always play out the same way.
