Train Your Monster ("the Game") is a single-player, offline monster-raising auto-battler.

## Data we collect
- **Gameplay progress** is stored **only on your device** (local save file). It is not
  transmitted to us.
- The Game does **not** require an account, sign-in, or personal information to play.

### Third-party services (only if enabled in this build)
This build includes the Unity engine, which may — depending on configuration — use:
- **Unity Analytics / Unity Services:** anonymous usage and device diagnostics
  (device model, OS version, coarse usage events) to improve stability.
- **Google Play Billing:** if in-app purchases are offered, purchase transactions are
  processed by Google Play; we do not receive your payment details.

If this build ships with Unity Analytics and Google Play Billing removed, the Game
collects **no data** and transmits nothing off the device.

## How data is used
Solely to operate and improve the Game (stability, crash diagnostics). We do not sell data.

## Children
The Game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from
children. See the content rating in the store listing.

## Your choices
You can clear all local progress by uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date reflects the latest version.
